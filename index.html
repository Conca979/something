LogicForge Quick Guide & Shortcuts

Mouse Controls

  • Place Component Click Palette & Click Canvas
  • Connect Wire Drag from Pin to Pin
  • Toggle Switch / Button Click Component Body
  • Pan Canvas Middle Mouse or Wheel Drag
  • Zoom In / Out Scroll Wheel
  • Multi-Select Drag Selection Marquee

Keyboard Shortcuts

  • Play / Pause Space
  • Step 1 Cycle S
  • Delete Selected Delete / Backspace
  • Undo / Redo Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y
  • Center / Fit View C
  • Cancel Wire / Deselect Esc
